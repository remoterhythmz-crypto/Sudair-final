## 2. Global findings (apply to every post)

### 2.1 Typography — three rules that must be enforced
The Foray benchmark (two-level Arabic headline, white line 1 + mint line 2, one kashida word, italic serif caps in English) is correctly followed in 49 of 54 images. The failures all come from one cause — **kashida (tatweel) stretching applied to the wrong words**:

1. **Never stretch a word containing ء/إ hamza or definite-article vowels** — stretching «الإفراج» produced the broken «الإقـراء» on JAN-F04.
2. **Never stretch more than one word, never a phrase** — JAN-F10 stretched a whole phrase and destroyed the headline («تفحَّص الشَّ فَ بِلَ…» is unreadable).
3. **Cap elongation at ~30% of the word's natural width, minimum 4 letters** — short words (نقف، مدد) distort or pick up stray diacritics (NOV-F04 «نقفٍ» carries a stray tanwin).

Additional Arabic correctness fixes:
- **NOV-F04:** remove the stray kasra/tanwin under «نقف» → «نقف معكم» plain.
- **JAN-F07:** «الألة» is missing the madda → **«الآلة تعمل، والإنسان حاضر»**.
- **DEC-F03:** the rendered headline reads «لإفريقنا» (extra hamza) — re-typeset to **«لفريقنا»** and proofread at full resolution.
- **JAN-S10:** headline «حصر الأسبوع بالصور» — the idiomatic word is **«حصاد»**; confirm before posting.

Latin type is consistent and on-brand everywhere (italic serif caps headline, light sans support, letterspaced). No change needed.

### 2.2 Style discipline
- **No cartoon/3D avatar characters.** The Foray designs and the brand guidelines are photographic/documentary. Five posts (NOV-F08, NOV-S05, NOV-S09, JAN-S02, JAN-S05) use 3D illustrated characters — they read as a different brand. Rebuild as photographic frames or type cards.
- **No stock-metaphor AI imagery.** DEC-F10's crystal ball is generic AI stock, no Sudair asset, no documentary value. Replace with the real golden-hour drone frame of the site (available: `DJI_20260121084222_0001_D.MOV`).
- **Story sticker zones** (dashed «STICKER ZONE» boxes) are intentional placeholders — keep, but ensure the live sticker (poll/question) is added natively on Instagram and the box is not visible in the final post.

### 2.3 Asset use
- The source-verified rebuild is correct: real frames, colour-graded to the emerald palette, no generated people or buildings in the 33 asset-based posts. ✅
- **Frame over-reuse:** `ALABBASY.8829.MP4` (masked lab colleague) now appears in NOV-F02, NOV-S11, NOV-F12, JAN-F11, JAN-S11 — five posts. Spread is tolerable across three months, but **JAN-F11 «Meet the quality team» is factually mismatched** (it is the lab team frame): either swap to a QC-lab frame (`ALABBASY.8839/8842`) or retitle to «فريق المختبر».
- **Construction-stage frames** (`DJI_20260121084636`) are fine for «Built here» storytelling (DEC-F01, JAN-F01) but weak as the hero for a capability message (DEC-F07). Swap DEC-F07's hero to the finished-facade frame (`DJI_20260121103516_0070_D.MOV`).
- Three posts are 100% generated with no asset (NOV-F06, DEC-F10, NOV-F08). All three are on the rebuild list and all three can be rebuilt from real footage that exists in the library — prefer that.

### 2.4 Value proposition ranking
- **Strongest:** NOV-F02 (people proof), DEC-F01 (site proof in four frames), NOV-F12 (recap composite), DEC-F06 (real team scale). These do the brand's job: capability you can see.
- **Weakest:** DEC-F10 (generic metaphor), NOV-F08 (cartoon), DEC-F07 (construction hero for a manufacturing message), NOV-F06 (Ramadan lantern in November — wrong cultural symbol for a night-shift thank-you).
- Recommendation: the Foray photographic direction should always win over illustrated/stock concepts when a real frame exists.
