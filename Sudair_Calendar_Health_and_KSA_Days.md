# Sudair Pharma — Social Media Content Calendar with World Health Days & KSA Important Days
**Window covered here:** November 2026 – January 2027 (the 3-month production pack) · **Part 1: content only, no design**
Design, prompts and assets follow in the separate sections/files (`Sudair_3Month_Prompt_Book.xlsx`, `Sudair_3Month_Still_Post_Prompts.md`, `Sudair_Carousel_All_Slides_Review.md`).
**Compliance baseline:** every item is written as solidarity/awareness/culture content — no efficacy, dosage, comparative or quality-assurance claims. January process pieces (marked ⚠) need client regulatory sign-off before posting.

## 1. Strategic frame (how the calendar is built)

| Layer | Rule |
|---|---|
| Primary channels | LinkedIn (corporate engine, 105K) · X (announcements) · Instagram + Facebook (culture/people, post Week-0 reset) |
| Pillar mix | Made here 25% · Meet the makers 25% · With you — care & moments 25% · Year & community 25% |
| Awareness days | Solidarity greetings only («نقف معكم» framing). No advice, no numbers, no disease imagery of patients |
| KSA days | National pride framing; never tied to product or capacity claims |
| Rhythm | Mon = capability · Wed = people · Fri = community (IG); Mon/Wed/Fri carousels on LinkedIn |
