# Sudair visual and content edits required

**Scope:** 54 selected still/Story draft images in `Generated_Stills/Source_Verified/`, 33 carousels and 9 Reels awaiting production. The deck uses these drafts for internal review; no image is represented as client-approved or publication-ready.

## Selection and asset decision

- For each concept that names a Sudair photograph or footage asset, select the source-verified rendition. Its photo panel contains a frame from the referenced real library file, not an invented building or person. The real footage remains the source of truth; preserve the photographer/employee/event usage rights record.
- For 21 concepts without a required real photograph, use the existing generated-only artwork as a visual direction. Verify people, objects, Arabic and English glyphs, logo placement and date before release.
- The selected composite treatment is deliberately restrained. It is accurate but repetitive: the narrow photo rectangle and fixed headline hierarchy need art-direction variants before a live three-month feed. Keep the team’s emerald palette and logo; alternate photo scale, crop and type-led compositions while retaining a coherent grid.
- Do not treat a photographed colleague as a specific department, shift or senior role unless Sudair identifies the person and approves the caption.

## Typography and layout corrections

1. Keep Arabic AE_Sharjah as editable text in the layout file, with right-to-left shaping reviewed by a native Arabic reader. Verify each kashida and diacritic; image-generation text is a draft only.
2. Keep the supplied Sudair white logo on dark artwork and the full-colour version on light artwork. Do not redraw or use a generated logo. Maintain clear space around it; the small logo in Story renders may be unreadable at phone size.
3. Use Friz Quadrata for English display, Roboto for supporting information, and the brand green palette. Check minimum type size after Instagram/X/LinkedIn compression; the small English sublines and footer on several tiles are currently too fine.
4. Keep critical Story words and logos clear of interface overlays at the top and bottom. The current Story safe area should be tested in a live mock-up.
5. Correct title/date divergence: NOV-F04 is now planned for 14 November, matching the image. DEC-F06 and several January images require on-image copy replacement after fact approval.
