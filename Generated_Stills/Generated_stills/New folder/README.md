# Sudair exact-size image concepts

This folder contains 54 one-to-one image concepts from `Sudair_3Month_Still_Post_Prompts.md`: 18 feed posts at 1080 × 1350 and 36 stories at 1080 × 1920. The official Sudair white logo or symbol PNG was placed as a separate layout asset after generation. Original renders and rejected variants remain in `../` for comparison.

These files are **internal creative review assets, not cleared publication masters**. Inspect Arabic spelling, letter joins, English copy, and the logo clear space at full resolution. AI may reconstruct source buildings or people; compare each against the real asset path in `../Selected/Image_QA_Manifest.csv`. Confirm written consent for recognizable people. The six January still/story concepts marked as held in the manifest require client fact and regulatory review. No image was placed into the presentation.

The contact sheets `nov_contact.jpg`, `dec_contact.jpg`, and `jan_contact.jpg` support quick review, but do not replace full-resolution checking.
