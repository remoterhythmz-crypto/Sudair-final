# Sudair client data request — concise version

**Purpose:** give the three-month corporate campaign specific, current and publishable evidence. Please return a fact sheet and matching photo/footage IDs, with an owner and public-use status for each item.

## Already published on Sudair’s website — confirm currency before reuse

| Topic | Published statement | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Site footprint | 72,000 m² site; 12,000 m² built area | Operations page |
| Oncology areas | 2,826.9 m² production; 1,848.6 m² storage; 70.3 m² refrigerated storage (2–8°C) | Projects page |
| Oncology line capacity | 300M tablets; 150M capsules; 9M sterile liquid units; 6M lyophilised vials, stated for one shift/day | Projects page |
| Projects | Insulin, biologics and respiratory facilities described in the roadmap | Projects page |

The site-area figures and oncology-area figures describe different scopes and must not be combined. The capacity numbers are **one-shift stated capacity**, not actual output. The website describes several facilities as projects; request the operating status of each before calling it operational.

[Operations page](https://sudairpharma.com/operations/) · [Projects page](https://sudairpharma.com/projects/)

## Please provide

### 1. Current site and departments

A dated map showing each operating, commissioning and planned building; current area for production, QC, QA, warehousing, utilities and offices. Reconcile the 72,000 m² website figure with any other figure used in presentations.

### 2. Lines and capacity

A one-page line table: dosage form, line/equipment name, installed and qualified status, rated capacity with time/shift denominator, latest actual output period, utilisation, and whether each number may be public.

### 3. Quality and technology

What QC, QA and validation teams do; approved photos of their actual spaces; the exact automation, containment, isolator, monitoring or digital systems in use, with vendor/name and public-use permission. Separate operating technology from planned technology.

### 4. People and partners

Names and roles of department heads, years of relevant experience, prior affiliations or qualifications that may be published, approved short quotes, portrait consent and partner-name/logo permission.

### 5. Image identification and rights

For each selected photo or footage file: facility, department, date, people depicted, photographer/owner, written release, and any restricted equipment, label, screen or third-party mark. Identify which first-wave images are illustrative and must not be presented as documentary Sudair photographs.

### 6. Sign-off and brand

Current master logo and brand guide, the approver for corporate facts and medical wording, and which facility/capacity/leadership facts are cleared for public social use.

## Minimum first response

1. Current facility/department map and operating-status legend.
2. Line-by-line capacity sheet with denominator and latest actual-output period.
3. QC/QA and technology fact sheet with approved images.
4. Leadership list with public bios and consent.
5. Asset-identification and rights register for the images used in the proposed posts.
